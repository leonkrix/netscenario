# D-054: The Kernel backend builds the loss models, the reordering and the duplication

Accepted (2026-11-01), on the ground of D-030, D-048 and D-052. Corruption follows (D-055) with its accounting (D-040), so the backend still refuses it, and the statistics (reordering extent, interarrival jitter, loss runs) after it (D-058).
- **The order of the stages is that of `netem_enqueue`**: duplication, loss, corruption, rate, delay with jitter and reordering. A direction is one `netem` queue, or two (the first with the duplication, the loss and the rate, its child with the delay, the jitter and the reordering) when it has a **rate and a jitter or a reordering**. The jitter was the reason in D-030. The reordering is new, and it was measured: a packet that `netem` chooses for reordering is put at the head of the queue and the rate is worked out only for the packets that are delayed, so in one queue a reordering lets a share of the packets past the rate. In S12.9 (a stream far above 2 mbit, 20,000 packets per second) 25.10 % of the packets got past the rate in `delay 10ms reorder 25% rate 2mbit`, against 0.01 % with the rate in a queue of its own in front (seeds 11, 22, 33; the figures of the three seeds are within 0.7 percentage points). The fidelity case `14-reorder-with-rate` holds the rate at the 2 mbit of frames.
- **The duplication is in the first queue.** `netem` enqueues the copy at the root of the tree, so a copy made in the second queue would pass the loss and the rate a second time (a unit test keeps it out of the second queue). The copy passes everything that follows on its own (D-048); the fidelity cases `12` and `13` hold the figures: 16.2 % of the packets arrive twice with a loss of 10 % and a duplication of 20 %, and 8.2 % are lost.
- **Seeds.** Everything that draws numbers the seed is to repeat is seeded: the first queue (loss, duplication) with the seed of the direction, the queue with the reordering with a seed derived from it (`derive_stage_seed`, pinned like the others, so that the two queues do not draw the same numbers); a delay with a jitter alone stays unseeded (D-030). In the core, a scenario depends on the seed for all of loss (every model), duplication, corruption and reordering (`Impairments::is_random`): `loss_reproducible` and the need of `tc` 6.6.0 follow that, and before this step a scenario with a duplication but no loss would have said `true` without a seed on the queue. The integration test `the_same_seed_repeats_burst_loss_duplication_and_reordering` runs a direction with a Gilbert-Elliott loss, a duplication, a reordering and a rate twice with one seed and once with another: the lost packets, the doubled ones and the ones sent on at once are the same in the first two and differ in the third.
- **The check of the queue (ENV-07) does not apply to a duplicating direction.** The check compares the packets that the queue counted with those that the workloads sent. The queue counts the copies it makes as well, and the number of copies cannot be told from a packet that nobody asked for, so the comparison would fail for every duplicating run or pass for any. The direction has no entry in `queue_checks` (documented in `RESULTS.md`); `loss_reproducible` stays `true` on the same condition as for a single workload. A stray packet in such a direction goes unnoticed; the alternative, saying `false` for every duplicating run, would say that the best case is unreproducible. A counter that names the copies would remove the limit (the kernel offers none).
- **The tree of two queues with a duplication** is accepted by the three kernels of the project (D-052, S15) and is asked of the kernel otherwise: `doctor` has the lines `models` and `duplicate-tree`, and the run refuses a direction that needs the tree where the kernel says no, with the kernel named and its answer quoted, before anything is built.
- **Fidelity (QA-06), eight new cases with canaries:** the Gilbert-Elliott loss (4.76 % and a mean run of 5.0 packets), the four-state loss (6.34 % and 1.77), the reordering (25 % sent on at once, 12.5 % with a gap of 5), the duplication (20 %, with a loss, and in a tree of two queues) and the reordering behind a rate. The canaries run a loss without bursts of the same mean (it passes the mean and fails the run length), a reordering and a duplication that are not applied or of another size, and a rate that is missing.
- **Left for the whole state:** a change over time must write the whole state of a direction with explicit zeros (D-048) and may need to add or remove the second queue (D-030); that is the timeline (D-059). `apply` here still builds a direction once, on a fresh interface.
