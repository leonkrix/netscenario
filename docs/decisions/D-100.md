# D-100: What the engine does in 0.4: the 25 cells of its own, ECN, the live change; queue disciplines last, and anything it does not do it refuses by name

Accepted (2026-11-12), for BKD-06, LNK-13 and TML-05. `docs/COVERAGE.md` has 53 cells `✔ 0.4` in the Engine column; 28 follow from the build of the Kernel backend (nodes are namespaces) and need the existing tests to run on the engine, 25 are the engine itself.

- **The 25:** the impairments (fixed delay, jitter in five distributions, independent and burst loss, reordering, duplication, corruption, rate in Ethernet frames, queue limit, MTU as the node's business, values per direction, outages), change over time (steps, down and up, traces, a rate that changes during the run at any width, models, periodic patterns), and the reproducibility of loss and of the due moments.
- **Rate steps of any width.** The engine has its own shaper, so the restrictions of the kernel (`fast-rate-steps` below 100 ms, `stalled-link`, D-091, D-092) do not apply to it and no `allow-limited` is needed; a test with a staircase of 1 ms steps has a canary that an averaging shaper fails.
- **The live change** (TML-05) is `✔ 0.4` for the engine: a change of a link from test code is a step of the timeline sent over the control channel (D-096), so the cell that said `✘` becomes a plan.
- **ECN** (LNK-13) is `planned` for 0.4, for both backends. S24 on the kernel: `codel`, `fq_codel`, `pie` with `ecn` and `netem loss ... ecn` mark an ECN-capable packet instead of dropping it (the counter `ecn_mark` and the CE count at the receiver agree to the packet: 63, 64, 22, 101), TCP with `net.ipv4.tcp_ecn=1` in the nodes sees the marks and needs no retransmission for them (`netem` with `ecn`: 0 retransmissions, without: 102); the key and the sysctl of the nodes are designed in the step of the queues. The Kernel cell `?` becomes `✔ 0.4`.
- **Queue disciplines last.** CoDel (RFC 8289), FQ-CoDel (RFC 8290) and PIE (RFC 8033) are the largest piece. They are built after everything else; if one is not ready when the rest is, it is refused by name, its Engine cell becomes `✘` with the reason, the ledger moves it to 0.5, and nothing is approximated. The decision is taken at the end of that step.
- **Refusals.** The engine declares what it can do as the Kernel backend does (`Capabilities`, D-023); what a scenario asks for and the engine cannot do is an error that names the feature, the backend and one that can. A cell stays `✔ 0.4` only when it links to a test that runs on the engine.

Alternatives: everything or nothing (a cut would be a surprise at the end); the engine without ECN (CoDel without the mark is half of CoDel); keeping the live change `✘` (the control channel makes it free).
