# D-084: The jitter takes its shape from a table of `netem`: the tables of iproute2, or one made from a file of samples, scaled to fit 16 bits

Accepted (2026-11-04). Release 0.3 completes the distributions of the jitter (LNK-02): `pareto`, `paretonormal` and `table`. D-083 says how the file of a table reaches the scenario.

- **What `netem` draws from.** The delay of a packet is `delay + amount x entry / 8192`, with an entry chosen at random from a table of 4,096 numbers of 16 bits. `normal.dist`, `pareto.dist` and `paretonormal.dist` of iproute2 are such tables (mean about 0 and deviation 1, 0.82 and 0.83; `pareto` is cut at 4 deviations above, `normal` at 3.5). `distribution: pareto` and `distribution: paretonormal` name them; the amount is the scale of the table as it is for `normal`.
- **`distribution: table` copies the shape of a file of samples.** `table: { file: delays.txt }`, one number per line (or several, separated by spaces or commas; `#` starts a comment), at least 100. The table is the 4,096 evenly spaced quantiles of the samples (the quantile at (i + 1/2) / 4096, a straight line between the two samples around it), moved to the mean 0 and scaled to the deviation 1. It is the shape only: the unit of the numbers does not matter, and `delay` and `amount` place and scale it (`amount` is the standard deviation of the delays, as for `normal`). The delay must be at least as many amounts as the table reaches below its mean (checked after the file is read), or the shortest delays would be below zero.
- **A long tail is scaled, not cut.** Entries are 16 bits, so a table holds 4 deviations (3.9999) at most. Samples that reach further (the delays of a queue have a tail) would be cut by a plain table, which is what the tables of iproute2 do and what makes their deviation 0.82. Instead the entries are scaled down by a factor (the `scale`, in thousandths, at most 16 because 4,096 entries cannot hold more than 64 deviations) and the amount that goes to `netem` is scaled up by the same factor, rounded to a microsecond. The delays come out with the deviation of the amount and the whole tail. Measured: a table from an exponential shape (reach 8.1 deviations, scale 2.024) gives a largest delay of 164.7 ms for `delay: 100ms, amount: 8ms`, where a cut table would stop at 132 ms.
- **`tc` finds a table of ours through `TC_LIB_DIR`.** `distribution NAME` reads `NAME.dist` from that directory (measured on iproute2 6.1; a path in `NAME` is not understood). The run makes a directory of its own the first time it needs a table, with the tables written there (named by their content, `ns` and 16 digits of a hash, so equal tables share a file and a step that changes only the amount names the same table) and with links to the tables of the system, and gives every `tc` of the run, the `tc -batch` included, that directory as `TC_LIB_DIR`. A batch that was started before is started again. The directory is removed at the end of the run; a run that was killed leaves one small directory in the temporary directory.
- **A step changes the amount and leaves the table.** In the timeline `jitter: { amount: 5ms, distribution: table }` takes the table of the link; a step cannot bring a table of its own (`netem` keeps the table loaded first, D-059). A step cannot switch a link to or from `table`, `pareto` or the others for the same reason.
- **The doctor probes** `normal`, `pareto` and `paretonormal` and a table in a `TC_LIB_DIR` of its own; a scenario that needs one the machine cannot load is refused before the network is built, with the answer of `tc`.
- **Fidelity.** Each case compares the mean, the median, the deviation, the smallest and the largest delay with the model of its table (bounds of four standard deviations from the table itself, the smallest and the largest as exact physical bounds); a canary shows that a normal jitter of the same mean and deviation fails the case of the table.

Alternatives: cut the tail like `maketable` of iproute2 (silently wrong for exactly the data a person has: round trips with a tail); refuse samples beyond 4 deviations (most real round trips); take `delay` and `amount` from the samples (a second way to say what the first already says, and no way to stretch a measured shape); a table file next to `tc` in the system directory (needs rights, and the host would be touched, SAF-01); `distribution` with a path (not understood by `tc`).
